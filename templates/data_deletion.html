Data Deletion Request

HangarLinks — Facebook Data Removal

If you signed in to HangarLinks using Facebook and would like your data removed from our platform, you have two options:

Option 1 — Email Us

Send a deletion request to privacy@hangarlinks.com with the subject line "Facebook Data Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account.

We will process your request within 30 days.

Option 2 — Delete Your Account

If you are logged in, you can delete your account directly from your Profile Settings page.

Alternatively, you can remove HangarLinks from your Facebook apps at facebook.com/settings → Apps and Websites. This will trigger an automatic deletion request to our servers.
