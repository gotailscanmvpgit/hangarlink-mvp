Pilot Arrival Guide

Flight Bag Ready: Essential steps for a safe arrival, parking, and tie-down at your HangarLinks destination.

Quick Start: 5 Steps to Perfect Arrival

1

Pre-Landing Review

Check your booking confirmation for exact gate codes, frequency info, and any "Private Access Instructions" from the host.

2

Follow the Blue Pin

The HangarLinks map pin is precision-set by the host. Taxi precisely to those coordinates to find your designated space.

3

Chock Before You Walk

Never leave your aircraft unchocked, even for a "quick" step into the FBO. High winds start suddenly.

4

Secure Tie-Downs

Use the standard HangarLinks knot (see diagram below). Avoid taut lines that put stress on airframe hardpoints.

5

Photo Confirmation

Snap a quick photo of your parked aircraft and message it to the host via the app. This builds trust and confirms arrival status.

Recommended for 1/2" - 3/4" Braided Line

Tail Height

Verify tail clearance against door header. If towing, ensure the tug path is clear of floor drains and ledges.

Safety Notification

HangarLinks hosts are independent owners. Always verify the structural integrity of tie-down rings and ground anchors before securing your aircraft. If you notice a hazard, report it via the app immediately.
